ABOUT THE ARTWORK

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, hundreds of times for every point. This one is Elephant Valley, where curling shapes like elephant trunks line up along the edge of the set. Zoom in on any part of the edge and you find more detail, forever. Maths can make beautiful things.
