ABOUT THE ARTWORK

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, hundreds of times for every point. This one shows a tiny copy of the whole set hanging on branching filaments. Zoom in on any part of the edge and you find more detail, forever. Maths can make beautiful things.
